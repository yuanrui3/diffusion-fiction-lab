回目 grading sheet

51 lines to grade. Each card shows the chapter summary and a couplet. One line is the original and was given to the model; the highlighted line was written by a model. Score the highlighted line for literary quality, 1 to 5, as a line of this couplet. Which model wrote it is hidden, and the order is shuffled.

Suggested anchors: 1 broken or meaningless · 2 reads, but flat or off the chapter · 3 acceptable · 4 good · 5 could stand as the chapter's 回目. Comments are optional. Your marks are saved in this browser as you go; press Export when done and put grades.json in results/grading/.
